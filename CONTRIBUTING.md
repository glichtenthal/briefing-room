# Contributing

This skill improves when people contribute realistic situations, failure cases, and examples.

Good contributions include:

- messy notes, transcripts, research dumps, or meeting-context scenarios that should produce better briefs
- examples where the skill summarized too much, advised too early, or missed uncertainty
- eval prompts with clear pass/fail expectations
- small wording improvements that make the skill easier for agents to invoke correctly

Please keep contributions focused on the skill's behavior. The goal is better judgment infrastructure, not more documentation for its own sake.

Open an issue with:

1. the situation or prompt
2. what the skill should do
3. what failure mode you want it to avoid
